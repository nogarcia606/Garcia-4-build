Book Synopsis

As a seventeen year old vampire, Kieran knows how to make his way into highschool. He is known to be handsome, artistic, and dress for style. Kieran, unfornanetly, keeps people at arm's length, until a new kid arrives into town. That new kid is Mason Kane and Kieran's opposite. As a sporty, playful, and obnoxiously funny boy, he is also the alpha werewolf's son. Kieran knows that he can't stand him and dislikes him to the very core. However as time progresses,their rivalry becomes more than just that and it seems that Kieran's feelings are shifting towards..love? Kieran was taught by his family that werewolves are the enemies of vampires and that they are to hate and fear them, but what if Kieran doesn't want to? Despite his clan's rules that threatens to destroy his and Mason's found relationship, Kieran must decide whether he can risk everything for a love that he was never supposed to have.

Main Characters

  • Kieran: A moody vampire boy who loves to do art and wants to just get past highschool
  • Mason: A werewolf boy who likes to go around and joke with everybody
  • Marcella: Best friend of Kieran and deeply supportive
  • Lara: A girl who enjoys hanging out with her friends
  • Annalisa: The cousin of Kieran and enjoys picking flowers
  • Cedric: Older brother of Kieran and mean when he's mad
